KnowlesCraft controller controls

Controller support is included on every computer. Connect a gamepad to switch to
controller prompts; disconnect it to return to keyboard/mouse mode. Controller
type is detected automatically. Prompts follow the connected controller, including on Steam Deck.

STANDARD GAMEPAD
Left stick: move; click to sprint. Right stick: look; click to toggle sneak.
A: jump. B: drop item / close menu. X: swap hands. Y: inventory.
RT: mine/attack. LT: use/place. LB/RB: hotbar. Menu: pause.
D-pad up: full map. D-pad down: backpack. D-pad right: voice settings.
D-pad left: pick block.

Hold View/Back, then:
Y: other mod controls ring (choose with stick, release to use).
X: perspective. A: screenshot. B: debug screen.
D-pad up: Create tool menu (hold); left: zoom (hold).
D-pad down: waypoint; right: chat.
LB: backpack tool cycle. RB: player list.

Inventories: A takes a stack, X takes one, Y quick-moves; B closes.
EMI recipe pages support the mod's LB+LT and RB+RT shortcuts.
Use the trackpad/mouse for recipe searching, maps and complex building screens.
All bindings are editable under Options > Controls > Controller.

STEAM DECK: STEAM INPUT (ONE TIME, FOR YOUR PRISM STEAM SHORTCUT)
Launch Prism from Gaming Mode. In Steam's controller settings select the template
"Gamepad with Mouse Trackpad". Keep the normal buttons/sticks/triggers as gamepad
inputs, rather than also sending WASD or mouse clicks (which causes double input).
Right trackpad: mouse; click: left mouse button.
Optional left trackpad: scroll wheel; click: middle mouse button.
Enable the back grip buttons and map L4 = V (voice), R4 = B (backpack),
L5 = Left Alt (Create tool menus), R5 = J (full map).
Hold L5 and scroll the left trackpad for Create menu selection.
Steam's own Steam+X shortcut opens its keyboard for chat/search.
The modpack cannot apply settings to an unrelated Steam shortcut for you.

DECK PERFORMANCE DEFAULTS
LCD (Jupiter): 60 FPS cap. OLED (Galileo): 90 FPS cap.
12-chunk view, 6-chunk client simulation, fast graphics, reduced particles,
clouds/shaders off, GUI scale 2. These are starting settings, not guaranteed FPS.
Server settings are unchanged. Defaults apply once; later personal changes stay.
Original options and Iris settings are kept under config/knowlescraft/.

Type /knowlescraft in game for help and your current keyboard bindings.
